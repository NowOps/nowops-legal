NowOps Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026 Last updated: November 25, 2026

NowOps helps restaurant teams have smoother, calmer shifts. Our aim is to help managers coach, teams train, and everyone stay motivated, without extra paperwork. To do that, we handle some personal information, and we take that responsibility seriously. This policy explains, in plain language, what we collect, why, who helps us, how long we keep it, and how you can reach us.

1. Who we are

NowOps ("NowOps", "we", "us") is based in British Columbia, Canada. It works as an operations co-pilot for quick-service restaurant franchise operators.

We provide NowOps to franchisee businesses (the "Operator"), who employ the restaurant managers and staff who receive our messages. In most cases, the Operator decides which people and stores use NowOps, and we handle personal information on the Operator's behalf to deliver the service.

2. What we collect

Depending on how an Operator uses NowOps, we may collect:

  • Contact details: WhatsApp phone numbers of restaurant managers and staff.
  • Work profile details: names, roles (for example manager, shift lead, team member), and which store and shifts a person works.
  • Message content: messages we send and messages people send back to us on WhatsApp, including replies, button taps, and the time they were sent or read.
  • Schedule photos: pictures of work schedules that managers send us so we can set up shift reminders.
  • Store operating data: store-level information such as sales, labour hours, inventory and food counts, cash and delivery-app reconciliation figures, and similar operating reports provided by the Operator or its systems.
  • Basic technical data: delivery and status information WhatsApp provides about messages (for example sent, delivered, read), and logs our systems keep for security and troubleshooting.

We do not ask for payment card details, government ID numbers, health information, or home addresses through WhatsApp. Please don't send them.

3. Why we use it

We use this information only to run and improve the NowOps service for the Operator, including to:

  • send daily shift briefs and routine operational updates;
  • send schedule reminders based on the schedules managers provide;
  • escalate issues to the right manager when something needs attention;
  • prepare operational reports for the Operator (for example store-level results and trends);
  • respond to questions and keep the service secure, reliable and working as intended.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for advertising or share it with advertisers.

4. Notice, consent and your choices

NowOps is used in a workplace setting. The Operator (your employer) provides us with the information needed to set you up, and is responsible for letting its team know that NowOps is used and why. We handle that information under Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and British Columbia's Personal Information Protection Act (BC PIPA), as they apply.

You can always:

  • ask questions about how NowOps uses your information, by contacting your manager or us (see section 12);
  • stop NowOps messages at any time. You can reply to any NowOps message asking us to stop, in your own words. We'll stop messaging you as soon as we see your request and let your store manager know. You can also ask your manager or the franchisee operator to stop them. Stopping messages doesn't delete your information; to ask for that, see section 10. Opting out of NowOps messages does not affect how your employer otherwise communicates with you about work;
  • ask to see, correct or delete your information (see section 9).

5. Who helps us (service providers)

We use a small number of trusted service providers to run NowOps. They may handle personal information only to provide their service to us, under contract:

  • Supabase: hosting and database, to store and process NowOps data.
  • Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform): to send and receive WhatsApp messages. WhatsApp's own terms and privacy policy also apply to your use of WhatsApp.
  • AI services: to read the schedule photos managers send us.

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect the safety of people or the security of the service. If NowOps is ever reorganized or sold, information may transfer to the new owner under the same protections.

6. Where your information is stored

Our main database is hosted by Supabase in Canada (Canada Central region). Some of our service providers process information outside Canada: Meta processes WhatsApp messages on its own infrastructure, which may be outside Canada, and the AI services that read schedule photos may also process them outside Canada. Supabase may handle limited support and operational data from other countries. When that happens, the information may be subject to the laws of that country, including lawful access by its authorities. We choose providers with strong security and contractual protections.

7. How long we keep it

We keep personal information only as long as we need it for the purposes above, or as the law requires:

Information Retention period
WhatsApp message content and replies We aim to keep these no longer than 12 months, and we delete them sooner on request (see section 10).
Schedule photos We aim to delete them within 90 days after processing, or sooner on request.
Names, roles and phone numbers While the person is an active user. After removal, or after a deletion request, we aim to delete them within 30 days, unless the law requires us to keep some information longer (see section 10).
Store operating data and reports For the length of the Operator's contract. After it ends, available to the Operator for export for 30 days, then deleted or de-identified within a further 60 days, unless the law requires us to keep it. Backups age out on their normal cycle.
Security and system logs Used for security and troubleshooting; we aim to keep them no longer than 12 months

When information is no longer needed, we delete it or make it anonymous. Today this is done by hand; automatic deletion is still being built.

8. How we protect it

We use reasonable safeguards for the sensitivity of the information. We use measures such as encryption in transit, encryption at rest (provided by our database host), access controls limited to people who need it, and multi-factor authentication on administrative accounts (we are still turning this on for some accounts). No system is perfectly secure, and if a breach creates a real risk of significant harm, we will notify affected people and the Privacy Commissioner as the law requires.

9. Your rights: access, correction and deletion

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you and how it has been used;
  • correct information that is wrong or incomplete;
  • delete your information or stop sending you messages.

Send your request in writing to the contacts in section 12. We may need to confirm your identity, and where we act for your employer we may coordinate with them. Section 12 explains when we'll respond and what to do if you're not satisfied.

10. Data deletion instructions

(The same instructions are on our standalone Data Deletion page.)

To ask NowOps to delete your data:

  1. Email privacy@nowops.ai, reply to any NowOps message on WhatsApp asking us to delete your data, or ask your manager or the franchisee operator to request deletion for you.
  2. If you ask through your manager or the franchisee operator, they'll send us your request in writing, with the WhatsApp number you use with NowOps, so we can find your records.
  3. We will confirm we received your request and aim to delete your personal information (phone number, name, role, messages, replies and any photos you sent) from our systems within 30 days. We'll let you know when it's done. If we must keep some information longer (for example, where the law requires it, or where it was used to make a decision about you that the law requires us to keep for a period), we'll tell you what we're keeping and why. We keep only a minimal record that you asked to stop (your NowOps ID, never your phone number) so you aren't messaged again.

Some information may also remain where it has been combined into store-level reports that no longer identify you. Deleting your NowOps data does not delete your WhatsApp account. To manage that, use WhatsApp's own settings.

11. Young people

NowOps is a workplace tool for businesses and is not directed at children. It is not intended for anyone under 16. Some restaurant team members employed by an Operator may be under 19. Where that is the case, we handle their information only for the same work purposes and with the same protections, and Operators are responsible for meeting any employment rules that apply.

12. Contact us and how to make a request

Who is responsible. Where we handle information on behalf of your employer (the Operator), the Operator is also responsible for it, and we may work with them to answer you.

How to reach us. Questions, requests or concerns about your personal information can be sent to:

  • Email: privacy@nowops.ai
  • WhatsApp: reply to any NowOps message.
  • Your manager or the Operator: they will pass your request to us in writing.

Requests are handled by our Privacy Officer (a role, not a named person). You can ask for the title and contact details of the person handling your request.

Making a request to see or correct your information. Please make your request in writing (email or WhatsApp is fine) and include enough detail for us to identify you and the information you're asking about. We may ask a simple question to confirm it's really you. There is no fee for requests about your own employee information.

When we'll respond. We will respond to a request to see or correct your information within 30 days of receiving it. If the law allows us more time (for example, if we need more detail to find your information, a large amount of information is involved, or we need to consult another organization), we may extend this. If we do, we'll tell you why, when to expect our answer, and how to complain about the extension. If we agree a correction is needed, we'll make it as soon as reasonably possible. If we don't make it, we'll note your requested correction on your record.

Deletion and stopping messages. See section 10 and our Data Deletion page.

Information handled outside Canada. Some of our service providers process information outside Canada (see section 6). You can ask us about this at the contacts above.

If you're not satisfied. You can ask the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner for British Columbia to review our response or to look into a complaint.

See also our Terms of Service and Data Deletion instructions.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as NowOps grows. When we make important changes, we'll update the date at the top and, where appropriate, let Operators and users know before the change takes effect.
