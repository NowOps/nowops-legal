NowOps Data Deletion Instructions

Effective date: November 25, 2026 Last updated: November 25, 2026

NowOps is an operations co-pilot for quick-service restaurant franchise operators. If you receive NowOps messages on WhatsApp, or your information has been added to NowOps by your employer, you can ask us to delete your personal information at any time.

How to ask

Choose whichever is easiest:

  1. Email privacy@nowops.ai.
  2. Reply to any NowOps message on WhatsApp asking us to delete your data.
  3. Ask your manager or the franchisee operator to send the request to us for you in writing.

If you ask through your manager or the franchisee operator, they'll pass on the WhatsApp number you use with NowOps so we can find your records. We may ask you a simple question to confirm the request is really from you.

What we delete

  • your WhatsApp phone number;
  • your name, role and the store and shifts linked to you;
  • messages we sent you and messages, replies and button taps you sent us;
  • any photos you sent us (for example schedule photos).

When

We'll confirm we received your request and aim to delete your personal information from our systems within 30 days, then let you know when it's done. Deletion is done by hand today. If we must keep some information longer (for example, where the law requires it), we'll tell you what we're keeping and why. We keep only a minimal record that you asked to stop (your NowOps ID, never your phone number) so you aren't messaged again.

Good to know

  • Some information may be kept longer where the law requires it, or where it has already been combined into store-level reports that no longer identify you.
  • After deletion you will no longer receive NowOps messages.
  • If you only want the messages to stop, you can reply to any NowOps message asking us to stop, in your own words. We'll stop messaging you as soon as we see your request and let your store manager know. You can also ask your manager or the franchisee operator. Stopping messages doesn't delete your data; to have it deleted, follow the steps in "How to ask" above.
  • Deleting your NowOps data does not delete your WhatsApp account. To manage that, use WhatsApp's own settings.

Questions

Email privacy@nowops.ai, reply to any NowOps message on WhatsApp, or ask your store manager or the franchisee operator. Section 12 of our Privacy Policy explains how requests work, when we respond and what to do if you're not satisfied. See also our Terms of Service.
